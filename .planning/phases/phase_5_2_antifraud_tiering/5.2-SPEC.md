# Phase Specification — 5.2: Anti-fraud & Tiering Engine

## 1. Goal
Implement a robust automated trust layer for FACILITA by enforcing physical geofencing, velocity limits to prevent fake volume, and a tiered provider system (Bronze, Prata, Ouro) that rewards performance with faster payouts and lower fees.

## 2. Requirements

### 2.1 Strictly Physical Geofencing
- **Current State**: Providers can start services without location validation.
- **Target State**: Mandatory location check at the moment of "Check-in".
- **Requirement**: The "Start Service" button in the Provider App must remain disabled if the distance between the provider's current GPS coordinates and the service address is **greater than 500 meters**.
- **Acceptance Criterion**: Provider attempts to check-in at 600m away and the button is unresponsive/shows error; at 450m, the button is enabled.

### 2.2 Velocity Anti-fraud Check
- **Current State**: No limit on services per day.
- **Target State**: Detection of unrealistic service volume.
- **Requirement**: If a provider completes **more than 20 services in a rolling 24-hour period**, all subsequent Payouts for that provider must be flagged as "SUSPICIOUS" and their processing blocked until manual Backoffice approval.
- **Acceptance Criterion**: Provider completes the 21st service; the associated payment is authorized but its Payout job in BullMQ is paused/blocked.

### 2.3 Provider Tiering System
- **Current State**: Single tier (implemented as D+15 placeholder).
- **Target State**: Three distinct tiers based on volume and quality.
- **Requirement**:
    - **Bronze**: Default level. Payout D+15, Fee 22%.
    - **Prata**: 10+ completed services AND rating > 4.5. Payout D+7, Fee 20%.
    - **Ouro**: 50+ completed services AND rating > 4.8. Payout D+2, Fee 18%.
- **Acceptance Criterion**: A Prata provider completes an order; the payout is scheduled for 7 days in the future with 20% commission deducted.

### 2.4 Daily Tier Calculation Job
- **Current State**: No mechanism to update tiers.
- **Target State**: Asynchronous daily update.
- **Requirement**: A background job (Cron/BullMQ) must run once every 24 hours to recalculate every active provider's Tier based on their lifetime performance metrics.
- **Acceptance Criterion**: A provider who reached their 10th service today is promoted from Bronze to Prata after the midnight job runs.

### 2.5 Backoffice Dashboard (MVP)
- **Current State**: No administrative interface.
- **Target State**: Secured management module within the Next.js PWA.
- **Requirement**: A dashboard at `/admin` (RBAC protected) allowing:
    1. Review and manual approval/rejection of Fraud Alerts (Velocity/Geofencing overrides).
    2. Visual review of service evidence (Photos and metadata).
    3. Manual override of a Provider's Tier.
- **Acceptance Criterion**: An operator logs in, sees a "Suspicious" payout (velocity check), and clicks "Release" to resume the payout flow.

## 3. Boundaries

### In Scope
- Geofencing validation logic (Backend + Frontend bridge).
- Velocity detection engine.
- Tier domain logic and fee/payout calculation mapping.
- Asynchronous Daily Tier update job.
- Administrative dashboard module (/admin).

### Out of Scope
- Support Chat integration.
- Admin-initiated password recovery (Self-service only).
- Discount coupon management.
- Detailed financial reports (Transaction list only).

## 5. Acceptance Criteria (Pass/Fail)
- [ ] Provider is physically blocked from starting service if > 500m from address.
- [ ] Payouts are automatically paused if daily service count > 20.
- [ ] Correct fee (22%, 20%, 18%) is applied based on Tier at the time of payment capture.
- [ ] Correct payout delay (D+15, D+7, D+2) is scheduled in BullMQ based on Tier.
- [ ] Admin can manually override a fraud block and a provider tier via `/admin`.
- [ ] Tier update job runs daily and updates provider statuses correctly.
