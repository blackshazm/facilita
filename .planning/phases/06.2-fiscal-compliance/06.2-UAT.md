# UAT — Phase 6.2: Fiscal Compliance & Tax Engine

## Overview
This document tracks the User Acceptance Testing and verification for Phase 6.2. The goal is to ensure that the fiscal engine correctly calculates taxes (including Reforma 2026), issues invoices via eNotas, and maintains an immutable financial audit log.

## Verification Checklist

### 1. Tax Strategy (CMP-01)
- [ ] **Current Tax Strategy**: Verify 20% commission calculation for current dates.
- [ ] **Reforma 2026 Strategy**: Verify IBS (17.7%) and CBS (8.8%) calculation for dates after 2026-01-01.
- [ ] **Edge Cases**: Zero amount, high volume transactions.

### 2. NFS-e Emission (CMP-02)
- [ ] **eNotas Adapter**: Verify mock emission and receipt of externalId.
- [ ] **Fiscal Listener**: Verify that `payment.captured` event triggers invoice creation.
- [ ] **Data Integrity**: Verify that `Invoice` record links correctly to the `Payment`.

### 3. Financial Audit Log (CMP-03)
- [ ] **Capture Audit**: Verify that a `FinancialAuditLog` entry is created after payment capture.
- [ ] **Data Immutability**: Verify that the log contains `before` and `after` states.
- [ ] **Actor Identification**: Verify that 'SYSTEM' or 'ADMIN' is correctly recorded.
