### Area: Reward Mechanics & Format
**Options Discussed:**
- Immediate vs Deferred rewards.
- Cash vs Promotional Credits.
- Slugs vs UUIDs for invite codes.

**User Selection / Decision:**
- **D+15 Deferral:** Rewards are scheduled for 15 days after the referee's first successful order.
- **Promo Credits Only:** Rewards are explicitly NOT withdrawable cash. They act as discounts for customers and fee subsidies for providers. Value is strictly R$ 10,00.
- **Slugs:** Invite codes will be human-readable slugs for better WhatsApp conversion.

**Notes:**
This protects the platform's cash flow by ensuring rewards are funded by actual service usage and not exploitable for direct withdrawals.

### Area: Anti-Fraud Boundaries
**Options Discussed:**
- Limits on referrals.
- Sybil attack detection (self-referrals).

**User Selection / Decision:**
- **Strict Cap:** Maximum 200 referrals per user. Admin override required for influencers.
- **Device & Payment Matching:** System will silently drop referrals if the `deviceId` matches the referrer's, or block at payment time if the credit card is shared.

**Notes:**
Leverages the existing `UserDevice` identity model.
